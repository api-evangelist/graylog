Examines Singapore's Cybersecurity Code of Practice 2.0, a legally binding standard for critical infrastructure owners that shifted from reactive to proactive monitoring across both IT and OT environments. Details six indicators of detection gaps and emphasizes that organizations must build continuous compliance into daily operations rather than treating it as point-in-time.
