Organizations generate terabytes of log data daily while struggling to determine which information to preserve and for how long. This article provides guidance on implementing tiered storage approaches for log data management, balancing compliance needs with infrastructure expenses through hot, warm, and cold storage strategies. The piece draws an analogy between household storage habits and how security teams manage digital logs to illustrate the trade-offs involved.
