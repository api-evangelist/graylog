Guide to Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) and North American Electric Reliability Corporation (NERC) cybersecurity requirements for energy sector organizations. Covers all 13 enforceable CIP standards, including system categorization, security management, personnel training, electronic security perimeters, physical security, system hardening, incident reporting, and recovery planning, along with the technical monitoring and detection capabilities needed for compliance.
